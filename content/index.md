**The unofficial wiki for all things IUJ. From your senpais, for you, our kouhais.**

Everything here was written by students who arrived before you and worked it out the slow way: the visa paperwork, the first Niigata winter, which SIM actually works before your Residence Card is registered. If it would have saved us a week, it belongs here.

> **This is an unofficial, student-led wiki.** It is not affiliated with, supported by, or endorsed by IUJ administration or staff. Procedures, fees, and deadlines change without notice, so confirm anything that matters through official IUJ channels before you act on it.

New here? It's completely normal to feel lost the first few weeks. Ask a senpai (a senior student); nobody expects you to work it all out alone. Need to get around? See [[Bus & Local Routes]] for schedules, or [[Useful Apps in Japan — Maps, Transit, Translation|Useful Apps]] for navigating the city.
