title: Country-Specific
---

Guidance for students from particular home countries: what to bring, what
you will miss, where to buy it here, and who else on campus is from the same
place.

Almost all of it is unwritten. These pages cannot be researched — no
official source knows which spice you should have packed or which festival
went unmarked last year — so each one waits for a student from that country
to fill it in.

## Your country isn't here

Use **✏️ Suggest an edit** on any page in this section and say which country
to add. A page will be created for it.

## Your country is here and it's empty

That is the normal state, and you are the person who fixes it. You do not
have to write the whole thing or write it well — one paragraph about food,
or a festival, or a group chat that new arrivals should know about, is
worth more than the empty page. It will be edited into shape for you.
