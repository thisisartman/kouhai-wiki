Setting up your dorm room in your first week: what's already there, what you'll want to buy, and where. Room dimensions and basic facilities are official; move-in day specifics (exact furniture, what's worth bringing) still need firsthand input. See Senior Submissions below.

---

## Room Dimensions

Per IUJ's own Dormitory Info:
- **SD1**: 14.3m², with a slightly larger unit bath than SD2/SD3. Ceiling height 2m50cm.
- **SD2 and SD3**: 15m², with a slightly smaller unit bath than SD1. Ceiling height 2m75cm.
- **MSA**: 2 unfurnished rooms (bedroom + kitchen/dining/living area) plus a small unit bath.

All single rooms (SD1-3) come with basic living facilities, a private bathroom, LAN + WiFi (personal routers recommended for stability), a peephole, and a chain lock. MSA is unfurnished. Bedding and table rentals can be arranged separately.

## Getting Started

- Once you know what's already in your room, you can plan what to buy locally. See [[100 Yen & Recycle Shops — Budget Furnishing]] for cheap furnishing options nearby.
- SD4 and MSA rooms may differ from SD1–3. See [[Campus Facilities & Dorms]] for what's known about each building.

## What to Buy Locally (First Few Days)

Per a senior submission — this is what to pick up nearby (Daiso, Seria, Can★Do, supermarkets) once you're on the ground, not what to pack from home. See [[Packing List]] for what to actually bring in your luggage; items below are the "buy after you land" counterpart, not a repeat of it.

**Bedroom**
- Bedsheet and set, if you don't want to use the one the dorm provides
- Hangers
- Laundry basket

**Kitchen**
- Frying pan or small pot
- Spatula and ladle
- Plate, bowl, mug
- Chopsticks, spoon, fork
- Knife and cutting board
- Dish soap and sponge
- Trash bags

**Bathroom**
- Bath towel, hand towel
- Toothbrush & toothpaste
- Shampoo & body wash
- Toilet paper
- Laundry detergent
- Toilet cleaning brush (if needed)

**Cleaning**
- Broom
- Antibacterial wipes
- Multipurpose cleaner

**Electronics**
- Extension board/power strip
- USB adapter (optional — see [[Packing List]] for what to bring instead)
- **Wi-Fi router.** The room WiFi can be patchy, and IUJ's dorm LAN guidelines allow a personal router plugged into the wall LAN port (the school shop sells them too). A senior bought a used Buffalo router at the Hard Off inside AEON Muikamachi for about ¥2,000, and it worked straight away. New ones cost roughly ¥5,000–7,500 at K's Denki (next to AEON Muikamachi) or Yamada Denki Muikamachi, or on Amazon. If the router has a ROUTER/AP switch, set it to AP. See [[Campus WiFi & LAN Connection]] for the LAN details.
- **LAN cable.** Borrow one from the MLIC office or buy one at the school shop; see [[Campus WiFi & LAN Connection]].
- **Laptop charger power cord.** Most laptop chargers have a detachable cord between the wall and the brick. Swap it for a Japanese-plug version rather than using a travel adapter. Check where the cord plugs into your charger first: three-hole "Mickey Mouse" (C5) or two-hole "figure-8" (C7). Buy the matching cord at K's Denki in Muikamachi, look in the Hard Off junk bins, or order it on Amazon. Not sure which one you need? Ask the IUJ IT helpdesk or a tech-savvy friend. Japanese sockets are 100V, which laptop chargers handle fine.

**Groceries (first 3–5 days)**
- Rice, eggs, bread, instant noodles, milk, yogurt, fruits, frozen vegetables
- Cooking oil, salt & pepper, tea/coffee
