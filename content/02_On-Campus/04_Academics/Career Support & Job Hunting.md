IUJ runs its own in-house career counseling and coaching service, aimed at individual attention given the small campus size — described by IUJ itself as unique among Japanese universities. Past IUJ students and interns have gone to companies including Accenture Japan, Google Japan, KPMG, Rakuten, Mizuho, Microsoft Japan, LINE, Ernst & Young, Société Générale, Mitsubishi Heavy Industry, Marubeni, and various UN agencies (UNDP, UNICEF) and national government offices — see the official guide for the full list.

---

## The IUJ Resume Book

IUJ publishes a **Resume Book** every November, distributing it by postal mail to companies and organizations in Japan and overseas, and directly to HR representatives who visit campus. An online version also exists (password required — check with Career Support for access). Registering for career support and building your entry in the resume book is a step in the process below, not optional if you want IUJ's recruiting coordination to work on your behalf.

---

## On-Campus Recruiting

IUJ invites companies and organizations to recruit directly on campus in Minami Uonuma City — typically an information session followed by same-day interviews. Video conferencing, phone, Skype, or Tokyo-based interviews can also be arranged for companies that don't want to travel to campus. There are three recruiting windows per year:

| Round | Timing |
|---|---|
| 1st | Early October – Mid November |
| 2nd (main recruiting period) | Mid January – Early March |
| 3rd | Early April – Mid June |

## Career Support Flow, Term by Term

| Term | What Happens |
|---|---|
| **Orientation (September)** | Career Guidance session (late Sept); New Students Welcome Day |
| **Fall (Oct–Dec)** | Career support registration; create your IUJ resume book entry; 1st recruiting round interviews; resume book published (Nov) |
| **Winter (Jan–Mar)** | 2nd recruiting round — the main recruiting period; individual consultation available as needed (including by email) |
| **Spring (Apr–Jun)** | 3rd recruiting round; two-year master's program graduation (late June) |
| **Summer (Jul–Aug, IMBA)** | Summer internships; one-year master's program graduation (late August); visa-changing process if staying in Japan for work |

Individual consultation (including by email) is available essentially year-round, not just during the three recruiting windows.
