IUJ offers a handful of optional add-on certificates layered on top of your regular degree — they're not required for graduation, but they give you a formal credential for a particular track of coursework if you complete the requirements. Two of them share a name ("Japan Focus") but come from different schools with different course pools, so don't assume the requirements are interchangeable.

---

## "Japan Focus" Certificate — GSIM

Source: GSIM Student Handbook 2025-2026, page 12, section III.E.

- Awarded on request at graduation to GSIM students who complete **8 credits** from a designated pool of **13 JGDP-track courses**.
- The exact list of 13 courses wasn't captured in the source used for this article — check the current list with the Registrar/OAA, since course offerings can shift term to term.
- **Students enrolled in JGDP itself are not eligible for this certificate** — they receive the JGDP credential directly instead.

> [!warning] Not the same list as GSIR's
> GSIR's Curriculum Handbook (page 14) has its own "Japan Focus" certificate with a separately-scoped course list. Same name, different school, different courses. If you're a GSIR student, the GSIM list above does not apply to you — check your own handbook.

## "International Organization Professionals (IOP) Focus" Certificate — GSIR

Source: GSIR Curriculum Handbook 2025-2026, pages 14-15.

- For students in the **IRP, IDP, PMPP, or IPPP** programs specifically.
- Draws from **12 designated courses**; you need to complete **5 of them** to earn the certificate.

> [!note] A similarly-named GSIM certificate also exists
> GSIM has its own, separately-scoped certificate for **ISEP** students under a similar name. This article doesn't have confirmed requirements for that version beyond the fact that it exists — don't assume it mirrors the GSIR IOP Focus requirements above.
