GSIM's (MBA/JGDP/IMBA/DXP/ISEP) graduation research process is genuinely different from GSIR's thesis process — see [[Thesis Guide (IR) — Supervisor, Timeline, Tips]] for that one. Don't assume the two are interchangeable. Which version applies to you depends on whether you're in a 1-year or 2-year GSIM program.

## 1-Year Programs (IMBA, DXP, ISEP): "Research Report"

Source: [Research Seminar Guidelines 2025-2026 (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/assets/img/ResearchReport-Thesis_Research_Seminar_Guidelines_for_2526enrollees.pdf), for students who enrolled in the 2025/2026 academic year.

- **No thesis option** — 1-year program students write a **Research Report** only.
- Required course sequence: **Research Seminar I (Winter) and II (Spring)**, 2 credits total, graded **Pass/Non-pass**.
- Supervisor assignment happens in **October**, matching students by research/teaching expertise area.
- Process: submit **Form 1** (tentative title + preferred supervisor choices) to the Office of Academic Affairs (OAA) → Dean's Office announces pairings (in principle, max 10 students per supervisor) → submit **Form 2** (final supervisor + title, with supervisor's approval signature) to lock it in.
- Format: the report can be a **Case Study**, **Feasibility Study**, or a more standard **Scholarly Study**; can be done individually or (with supervisor recommendation + Dean approval) as a team.
- **Minimum length: 30 double-spaced typewritten pages** per individual contributor, excluding the bibliography.
- **2026 graduate deadlines** (verify against the current year's schedule each year, since these shift):
  - Submit to supervisor + OAA: **July 16, 2026**
  - Final clean version to OAA, both physical and electronic: **August 10, 2026, by 4:30pm**
- The physical copy needs your supervisor's signature and gets bound (**~¥3,800/copy**, paid by the student) and permanently kept in MLIC.
- OAA runs submitted reports through a plagiarism/generative-AI-use check before sending results to supervisors.
- JICA-JDS sponsored students and Linkage students must write a single-authored paper per their original application terms, regardless of the team option above.

## 2-Year Programs (MBA, JGDP): "Research Report" or "Thesis"

Source: [Advanced Seminar Guidelines 2024-2025 (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/assets/img/ResearchReport-Thesis_Advanced_Seminar_Guidelines_for_2425enrollees.pdf), for students who enrolled in the 2024/2025 academic year — this is the only version currently linked from IUJ's site; **confirm a 2026/2027-enrollee version doesn't exist before relying on the exact dates in this section**, since the structure below is a year old.

- 2-year GSIM students choose between a **Research Report** (recommended for most students) or a **Thesis** (for those pursuing research careers) — the thesis option demands a higher level of original academic contribution; the two have different evaluation criteria.
- Required course sequence: **Advanced Seminar I, II, and III**, 6 credits total across three terms, graded **Pass/Non-pass**.
- Supervisor assignment happens in **February of Year 1**; seminars begin the following Spring term of Year 1.
- Same Form 1 → pairing announcement → Form 2 process as the 1-year track above (max 10 students per supervisor, in principle).
- The **team option is only available for Research Report, not for Thesis** — thesis is always an individual project.
- Format options mirror the 1-year track: Research Report can be Case Study, Feasibility Study, or Scholarly Study; Thesis is always Scholarly Study.
- JICA-JDS sponsored students and Linkage students: same single-authored-paper requirement as above.

> ⚠️ This section doesn't have confirmed 2026-graduate-specific deadlines (submission dates, supervisor-change due dates) — the source PDF's dates are for 2024/2025 enrollees. Don't rely on specific dates here; check the current academic year's schedule with OAA directly.

## Common to Both Tracks

- In principle, only **full-time GSIM faculty** can supervise. Co-supervision by a visiting or GSIR faculty member is possible but must be noted in your proposal.
- You may change your research topic after your supervisor is finalized (with their support) without needing to report it to OAA — except JICA-JDS/Linkage students, who need sponsor permission for changes beyond minor tweaks.
- You generally **cannot change supervisors after submitting Form 2**, except with a formal "Application for Changing Supervisor" approved by both the current and new supervisor, by a hard deadline (check the current year's schedule).
- It's your responsibility to keep communicating with your supervisor and meet deadlines regardless of where they're physically based (on leave, off-campus, etc.) — this isn't waived just because your supervisor is hard to reach.
