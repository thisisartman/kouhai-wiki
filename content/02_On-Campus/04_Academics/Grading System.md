## Overview

Your grading system depends entirely on which school you're in — GSIM and GSIR run genuinely different grading structures, not variants of the same one. GSIM grades qualitatively (letter grades); GSIR grades numerically (0–100). The academic-warning GPA thresholds also differ between the two schools. See [[Course Registration]] for the broader registration context these grades sit inside.

---

## GSIM (MBA / JGDP / IMBA / DXP / ISEP)

Source: [GSIM Student Handbook 2025-2026 (PDF)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/assets/img/student-handbook_Handbook2025-26.pdf), pages 14–16.

### Grade Scale

Letter grades: A, A-, B+, B, B-, C+, C, C-, D, F

Non-letter grades:
- W (Withdrawal)
- I (Incomplete)
- P (Pass)
- D (Deficient) — the handbook lists this alongside the letter grade D; it's unclear from the source whether this duplicates the letter grade or denotes something distinct. Flagging as ambiguous rather than guessing.
- HD
- WA
- IP (In Progress)
- AU (Audit)
- NP (No Pass)
- RD (Report Delayed)

Some of these codes (HD, WA) aren't expanded in the source material available for this article — listed as-is rather than guessed at.

### Course-GPA Guardrails

Instructors are expected to grade within official target GPA ranges per course:
- **Core courses**: 3.00–3.50
- **Elective courses**: 3.00–3.60

### Academic Warning

Cumulative GPA below **2.5** triggers an academic warning.

### Withdrawal Deadline

End of week 4 of the term.

### Repeat Courses & Incomplete ("I") Grades

The GSIM Student Handbook covers repeat-course policy and the procedure for resolving an Incomplete ("I") grade, but the exact mechanics weren't captured for this article.

## GSIR (IR / IRP / IDP / PMPP / IPPP)

Source: [GSIR Curriculum Handbook 2025-2026 (PDF, updated Aug 2026)](https://www.iuj.ac.jp/assets/img/Academics-GSIR-CurriculumHandbook_2025.pdf), page 18 (Grading System) and pages 1–2 (Academic Performance Review).

### Grade Scale

GSIR uses a numeric 0–100 scale rather than GSIM's letter grades, e.g.:
- **A** = 96–100
- ...
- **F** = below 60

### Academic Warning

Cumulative GPA below **3.0** triggers an academic warning — a stricter threshold than GSIM's 2.5. This is a real, consequential difference: a GPA that would be fine standing in GSIM could trigger a warning in GSIR.

### Withdrawal Deadline

End of week 4 of the term (same as GSIM).
