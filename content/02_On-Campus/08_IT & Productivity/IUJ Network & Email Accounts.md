Your IUJ network account and email account are separate, managed separately, and expire on different terms. Worth understanding before you're stuck at graduation.

---

## Registration

If you already registered for a network account before arriving (including IEP participants), you can use WiFi and public PCs (PC120, PC121, SD2, SD3) immediately on arrival. If not, register in person at the **MLIC computer section, MLIC 3F**.

---

## Email Account

- Runs on Google Workspace for Education (Gmail): **gmail.com**, login `xxxxx@iuj.ac.jp`
- Webmail recommended for most students; if you need to configure a desktop/mobile mail client instead, contact [MLIC IT Services](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/cs/) for current server settings
- MLIC also has an official guide for [enabling two-step verification](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/cs/manuals/two-step-verification/) on your IUJ Gmail — worth doing, this account holds your entire academic life

## Network Account

The part of your email before `@iuj.ac.jp`. Used to log into public PCs, **MyIUJ**, **DreamCampus**, the Library E-Resource service, and network printing.

## Google Drive

10GB included with your IUJ account (shared across Gmail, Drive, Photos). Check usage at [drive.google.com/settings/storage](https://drive.google.com/settings/storage) — if you exceed the limit, you stop being able to save files *and* receive email.

---

## After You Leave IUJ

| Account | Regular students | Non-regular students |
|---|---|---|
| **Email** | Stays active indefinitely — but deleted permanently if not logged into for 1 year | 1 month, then erased |
| **Network account** (portal, e-resources, printing) | 1 month for everyone, regardless of student type | 1 month |

> ⚠️ If you're a regular student planning to keep using your `@iuj.ac.jp` address as alumni, log in at least once a year. There's no recovery once it's auto-deleted.

---

## Changing Passwords

Network and email passwords start identical but are **changed separately** — updating one doesn't touch the other.

**Network password**: on a public PC, Ctrl+Alt+Del → Change a password → enter old password, then new password twice → confirm.

**Email (Gmail) password**: log in at gmail.com → Google Account → Security → Password (under "How you sign in to Google") → re-enter current password → set new password.

> ⚠️ If your personal laptop's local Windows account name matches your IUJ account name, keep both passwords identical — otherwise Windows gets confused about which credentials to use for the network. Also never name a local account "admin"; it causes IUJ network conflicts.

## User Support

- **IT Helpdesk**, MLIC 1F — student support team, check posted consulting hours
- **MLIC Office**, MLIC 3F — weekdays 8:30–16:30
- Contact details and current hours: [MLIC IT Services](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/cs/)
- MLIC doesn't repair physically broken hardware — contact the manufacturer for that; Japanese-language support available if needed
