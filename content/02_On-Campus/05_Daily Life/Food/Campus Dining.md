## The Cafeteria: IUJ Shokudo

IUJ's on-campus cafeteria is called the **IUJ Shokudo** (食堂). It's open not just to students but to the general public — source: [IUJ Shokudo review, Japan Travel](https://en.japantravel.com/niigata/iuj-shokudo/60346).

**Term-time hours** (per the official IUJ menu page and the review above; confirm current hours before relying on these — the Confirmed: Hours Change Seasonally section below shows they shift during breaks):
- Lunch: 11:30–13:30 (last order 13:15)
- Dinner: 17:30–19:00 (last order 18:45)
- Days closed: not confirmed by either source — check current campus notices

**Menu and pricing**: the menu rotates daily with a mix of Japanese and international dishes — official examples include Grilled Salmon Assort Plate (¥600), Chicken Diavolo (¥550), Mala Tang (¥750), Phad GaPrao (¥750), Spaghetti all'Aglio, Olio e Peperoncino (¥650), Beef Yakiniku Plate (¥750). Source: [official IUJ menu page](https://www2.iuj.ac.jp/atiuj/menu/), which lists a ¥500–750 range per meal — a separate public review of the cafeteria described prices as generally staying around/under ¥500 with sizeable portions, so treat ¥500–750 as the current reference range and expect it may run a bit lower for some items.

**Halal**: the cafeteria offers halal-marked dishes on the rotating menu, and per the review above, staff are trained in halal food preparation to prevent cross-contamination — a genuinely useful fact for Muslim students that wasn't documented anywhere on the wiki before.

**Vegetarian/vegan**: not confirmed by either source above — still needs senior input, see [[Vegetarian & Vegan in Rural Niigata]] for general (non-cafeteria-specific) coverage.

## Confirmed: Hours Change Seasonally

Per an Office of General Affairs email (June 29, 2026), the IUJ cafeteria's lunch/dinner hours are **not fixed year-round** — they get shortened during the summer break period. As one dated example (not a permanent schedule, don't rely on these exact times): in summer 2026, lunch shrank to a narrow 11:30–13:30 window with dinner closed most weekdays, briefly reopening dinner (12:00–19:00) on a Saturday, then settling into a reduced dinner slot (17:30–19:00) from around July 9 through the rest of the break. The attached School Shop kept its usual 8:00–19:00 hours throughout. Takeaway for readers: **expect reduced cafeteria hours during summer/winter breaks and confirm current hours via campus notices**, don't assume term-time hours apply. `unverified` for anything beyond this general pattern.
